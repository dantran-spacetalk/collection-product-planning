spacetalk-faq.liquid — 5 questions, AIO + SEO optimised

Good to know

  • Are Spacetalk watch straps compatible with both the Adventurer 2 and Loop?

    Yes. All Spacetalk Band Straps — both the single strap and the 3 Pack Bundle — are designed to fit both the Adventurer 2 and Loop. They share the same lug width and attachment system, so you can swap straps between models without any adapter.

  • Do Spacetalk watches work with any SIM card?

    No. Spacetalk Adventurer 2 and Loop watches require a Spacetalk SIM card and mobile plan, which runs on the Telstra 4G network. Standard consumer SIM cards from other Australian carriers are not compatible with Spacetalk devices.

  • What accessories come in the box with a Spacetalk watch?

    The Adventurer 2 comes with a charging stand and USB cable. The Loop comes with a magnetic charging cable. Screen protectors, additional straps, and SIM cards are all sold separately.

  • Can I use my Adventurer 1 charging stand with the Adventurer 2?

    Yes. The Spacetalk Charging Stand Kit is compatible with both the Adventurer 1 and Adventurer 2. If you already own the original stand, you don't need to buy a new one for the Adventurer 2.

  • How many colours are available for Spacetalk watch straps?

    Spacetalk Band Straps come in 12+ colours including Arctic, Black, Candy, Cherry, Lilac, and Lemon. The 3 Pack Bundle lets you pick any three colours at once, making it the most cost-effective way to build a strap collection.
