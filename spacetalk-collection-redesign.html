Getting started

Up and running
in 5 minutes.

No technical knowledge needed. Just follow four simple steps.

1
Charge the watch
Place on the magnetic charger for 30–40 mins before first use. A full charge takes about 2 hours.
2
Download the app
Get the free Spacetalk App on iOS or Android. Create your family account — takes 2 minutes.
3
Insert & activate the SIM
Your Spacetalk SIM is included. Slide it in and activate in the app. Allow 10–30 minutes for the Telstra network to activate.
4
Pair & personalise
Scan the QR code in the app to pair the watch. Add contacts, set School Mode hours, and draw your safe zones. Done.
⏱️
SIM activation takes 10–30 minutes. Once inserted, your SIM activates automatically on the Telstra 4G network. You'll receive a confirmation notification in the Spacetalk App once it's live. The watch can't make calls until activation is complete.

Kids GPS Smartwatches in Australia — Spacetalk

Spacetalk is Australia's leading kids GPS smartwatch brand, designed for families who want their children to have real-world independence without the risks of a smartphone. Every Spacetalk watch includes real-time GPS tracking, 4G calling on the Telstra network, SOS emergency alerts, and School Mode — with no internet browser and no social media of any kind.

The Spacetalk Adventurer 2 is a full-featured 4G smartwatch for kids aged 6–14, with HD video calling, a 5MP front-facing camera, and precise GPS location tracking. It's the school-safe watch with everything a growing child needs for independence. The Spacetalk Loop is a simpler first smartwatch designed for younger children aged 4–10, offering all the essential safety features — GPS, calling, SOS, School Mode — in a lighter design with longer battery life.

Both models run on Australia's Telstra 4G network and are managed through the free Spacetalk App, available on iOS and Android. Parents can approve contacts, set geofence safe zones, activate School Mode, and see live location from anywhere. Both watches are IP68 rated, durable, and built for daily school and outdoor use.

Whether you're searching for a school-safe kids smartwatch, a 4G GPS watch for your child, or an affordable first smartwatch with parental controls, Spacetalk has a model and bundle to suit your family.
