Questions About Our Kids Smartwatch Bundles

  • What is the best kids smartwatch bundle in Australia?

    The Adventurer 2 Smart Bundle is Spacetalk's best-value kids smartwatch bundle in Australia. It includes the Adventurer 2 — Australia's #1 kids smartwatch — plus a Spacetalk Watch Plan on the Telstra 4G network and your choice of strap colour, all for $266.99 (save $129.95). It comes with real-time GPS tracking, 4G calling, SOS emergency alerts and School Mode built-in.

  • Do kids smartwatch bundles include GPS tracking?

    Yes. Every Spacetalk kids smartwatch bundle includes a GPS-enabled watch. Both the Adventurer 2 and Loop feature always-on GPS location via the Spacetalk App, geofence safe zones with arrival and departure alerts, and one-tap SOS with live location. The Smart Bundles include a Watch Plan so GPS tracking is active from the moment your child puts the watch on.

  • Which bundle should I choose — Adventurer 2 or Loop?

    For kids aged 6–14 who want the full experience — HD video calling, a 5MP camera, messaging and full GPS tracking — choose an Adventurer 2 bundle. For kids aged 4–10 who need a simpler first kids smartwatch with GPS, 4G calling and SOS in a lighter design, the Loop Smart Bundle is the better fit. Both run on Telstra's 4G network and are managed through the free Spacetalk App.

  • What is the difference between the Smart Bundle and the Essential Bundle?

    The Smart Bundle includes the watch, a Spacetalk Watch Plan (with SIM card) and your choice of strap colour — everything to activate GPS, 4G calling and SOS on day one. The Essential Bundle includes the watch, a Charging Stand and a Black strap, but no plan, so you add one separately. The Adventurer 2 Smart Bundle saves $129.95 vs buying items individually.

  • Does the Smart Bundle come with a SIM card and mobile plan?

    Yes. Both the Adventurer 2 Smart Bundle and Loop Smart Bundle include a Spacetalk SIM card and a Watch Plan on the Telstra 4G network. Insert the SIM, activate via the Spacetalk App, and your child can make GPS-tracked calls, trigger SOS alerts and be located in real time — all on the same day as delivery.

  • Can I choose the watch colour when ordering a bundle?

    Yes. All three kids smartwatch bundles are available in Frost and Dusk. The Smart Bundles also let you choose your strap colour from the full range at checkout. The Essential Bundle comes with a Black strap as standard. All colour variants are shown on the product page with swatch selectors.
